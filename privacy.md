# Privacy Policy for FrameMe

FrameMe respects your privacy.

- **Data Collection**: This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data, browsing history, or images.
- **Local Processing**: Your Meural Canvas IP address is stored locally in your browser's sync storage to communicate with your frame.
- **Network Activity**: Images are fetched from the source website and sent directly to your Meural Canvas frame via your local network. No data ever leaves your local environment to reach the developer or third parties.
- **Permissions**: The `<all_urls>` permission is used strictly to allow the background script to download image files from the websites you visit when you select the "FrameMe" context menu option.

By using this extension, you acknowledge that all data processing occurs locally on your machine and network.
